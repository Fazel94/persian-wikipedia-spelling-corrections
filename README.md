# Persian (Farsi) Wikipedia Spelling Corrections

Real Persian spelling errors and their fixes, mined from the full edit history of Persian
Wikipedia (fa.wikipedia.org). Each fix is a single word that a human editor replaced with a
corrected form, kept with the sentence before and after the edit.

- **349,350** word fixes (`edits`) from **148,725** distinct typo → fix pairs (`pairs`).
- **12,915** pairs marked `confidence=high`: non-word typos confirmed by at least two
  editors (1,289 of them in `test`). Hand-checked precision on 200 random pairs: **98.5%**
  (95% CI 95.7–99.5%, see [Validation](#validation)).
- Errors were made by people typing, not generated by a noise model.

| typo | fix | edits | editors |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `برگذار` | `برگزار` | 463 | 136 |
| `حاظر` | `حاضر` | 354 | 138 |

### `pairs` columns

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `wrong`, `right` | the word before and after the edit (normalized, see below) |
| `edits` | revisions that made this replacement |
| `pages`, `editors` | distinct articles and distinct editors (accounts or IP addresses) |
| `first_year`, `last_year` | year of the first and last such edit |
| `summary_flagged` | edits whose summary mentions spelling (`املا`, `غلط`, `تایپ`, `typo`, …) |
| `reverse_edits` | edits replacing `right` with `wrong` |
| `wiki_freq_wrong`, `wiki_freq_right` | occurrences in the current text of all articles |
| `kind` | `zwnj` if the two differ only by ZWNJ (half-space), else `word` |
| `confidence` | `high` or `low`, see [Filtering](#filtering) |

### `edits` columns

`rev_id` (open `https://fa.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?diff=<rev_id>` to see the edit),
`page_id`, `timestamp`, `wrong`, `right`, `before` and `after` (the edited sentence, wiki markup
stripped; empty when longer than 500 characters), `editor_type` (`user` or `ip`),
`summary_flagged`, `kind`, `confidence`.

### Splits

About 10% of pairs go to `test`, chosen by a hash of the corrected word, so no corrected word
appears in both splits. Every edit follows its pair.

## Methodology

Source: the MediaWiki Content History export of Persian Wikipedia, snapshot 2026-11-01
(`dumps.wikimedia.org/other/mediawiki_content_history/fawiki/2026-11-01/`), 54 parts, 5.5 GB
compressed; every revision of every article is read. The XML is decompressed as a stream and
never stored. Word counts for the filter come from the last revision of each article (252
million tokens).

For each pair of consecutive revisions of an article (namespace 0), the edit is a candidate when:

1. **Small edit.** The page size changes by at most 8 bytes.
2. **Human, not a revert.** The editor's name does not mark a bot (`bot`, `ربات`), and the edit
   summary is not a revert or undo (`واگردانی`, `خنثی`, `undo`, `revert`, `rv`, …).
3. **Not reverted, not a revert.** The new text's SHA-1 does not match an earlier revision of
   the page (that would be a revert), and the edit is dropped if a later revision restores the
   text from before it (vandalism and its cleanup would otherwise produce reversed pairs).
4. **At most 3 changed lines.** Common leading and trailing lines are trimmed; larger rewrites
   are skipped.

The changed lines are split into sentences, aligned, and each aligned sentence pair is
tokenized into Persian words. Every one-to-one word replacement with a Damerau-Levenshtein
distance of at most 2 is kept.

**Normalization.** Both words pass through the same normalizer: Unicode NFKC; Arabic `ي`/`ى`/`ك`/
`ة`/`ۀ` become Persian `ی`/`ی`/`ک`/`ه`/`ه`; diacritics, tatweel and bidi marks are removed; ZWNJ
runs collapse to one, and a ZWNJ that cannot affect joining is dropped. Edits that only change
these characters are therefore not in the dataset. `before`/`after` keep the original characters.

### Filtering

`confidence=high` marks pairs that look like non-word spelling errors. All must hold:

- made by at least 2 distinct editors (drops one editor's renames across many pages),
- `reverse_edits` is under a third of `edits` (drops style disputes such as `او ↔ وی`),
- `kind` is `word`,
- the typo occurs under 100 times in current articles, and the fix at least 20 times and at
  least 10 times as often as the typo (drops content edits between real words, such as
  `یک → دو`),
- for words of up to 3 letters, typo and fix differ by one edit,
- not an ezafe style change such as `درجه‌ی → درجه`.

Everything else is `low`. `low` still holds many real errors, including real-word errors
(a valid word used in place of another), which need sentence context to judge.

## Validation

Two hundred `confidence=high` pairs were drawn at random (seed 0) and labelled by a native
Persian speaker as a spelling fix or not, looking at the sentence before and after.
197 of 200 were spelling fixes: **98.5%** precision (Wilson 95% interval 95.7–99.5%). The three
that were not: a name transliteration (`ماینکرافت → ماینکرفت`), a choice between two valid
spellings (`سپیدپوستان → سفیدپوستان`) and a name split (`کیی → کی`, from `بان کی مون`). Labels are in
`validation_sample.tsv` (`label` = `fix` or `not_fix`), each with a link to the diff.

Single annotator; no inter-annotator agreement was measured. Precision of `confidence=low`
was not measured.

## Limitations

- **Wikipedia register.** Formal, encyclopedic text. Colloquial typing errors (chat, phone
  keyboards) are under-represented.
- **Style and name edits remain.** Some `high` pairs are orthographic conventions or
  transliteration choices, not errors: hamza spelling (`موثر → مؤثر`), joined prefixes
  (`بطور → به‌طور`), foreign names (`آینشتاین → اینشتین`). A few renames by two editors also
  pass, e.g. `کوخری → کوخردی` (155 edits on 151 pages). Decide per use case.
- **Fixed errors only.** Typos nobody corrected are not here, and frequent errors are counted
  many times; use `editors` or `pages` to weigh them.
- **Single-word replacements only.** Split and merged words (missing or extra spaces) and
  multi-word rewrites are not extracted.

## Privacy

Only public data is used. Usernames and IP addresses are not published; `editors` is a count.
Each row's `rev_id` links to the public edit on Wikipedia.

## Reproduce

Code: https://github.com/Fazel94/persian-wikipedia-spelling-corrections (`fadict/`, taken from
the author's Persian keyboard dictionary project).
Python ≥ 3.10, `pip install regex`, and the `7z` command.

```sh
scripts/fetch_wikihist.sh 2026-11-01                    # 5.5 GB into data/raw/wikihist/
python -m fadict.wikiedits data/raw/wikihist/*.7z       # one worker per core
python -m fadict.wikiedits release                      # writes dist/fawiki_typos/
```

On 8 cores: about 20 minutes to download and 45 minutes to scan.
